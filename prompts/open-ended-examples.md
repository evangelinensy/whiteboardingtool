# Open-Ended Design Prompts (Interview Style)

These prompts follow the real interview format: very open-ended with one strong constraint. Based on actual design interview structure.

---

## Slack VR Integration

**Prompt:**
You're the CEO of Slack and want to introduce VR into the product. Figure it out.

**Constraint:** Must work with existing Slack workspaces and not require users to rebuy hardware

**Context:**
- Slack has 10M+ DAU across desktop and mobile
- Most teams use Slack for async communication
- VR adoption is still nascent (~5% of workforce has headsets)

---

## Uber for Elderly Users

**Prompt:**
Uber wants to serve elderly users (65+) who don't use smartphones. Design a solution.

**Constraint:** Cannot require the elderly user to own or operate a smartphone

**Context:**
- 30% of 65+ demographic doesn't own a smartphone
- This demographic represents significant untapped TAM
- Must integrate with existing Uber driver network

---

## Netflix Watch Party for Long Distance

**Prompt:**
Netflix wants to help long-distance couples feel more connected through shared viewing. Design the experience.

**Constraint:** Must work across different Netflix subscription tiers (Basic, Standard, Premium)

**Context:**
- Long-distance relationships represent ~14M US couples
- Existing "watch party" solutions lack intimacy
- Cannot require additional hardware purchases

---

## LinkedIn Job Search for Career Changers

**Prompt:**
Design a feature that helps people successfully transition into completely different careers.

**Constraint:** Cannot rely on traditional resume/experience matching algorithms

**Context:**
- 50% of workers are considering career changes post-pandemic
- Traditional job matching penalizes career changers
- LinkedIn has rich data on skills, connections, and content engagement

---

## Spotify Discovery for Niche Genres

**Prompt:**
Spotify wants to help listeners discover music in ultra-niche genres (< 10K monthly listeners). Make it happen.

**Constraint:** Must be profitable (can't just subsidize niche artists indefinitely)

**Context:**
- 90% of Spotify streams go to top 1% of artists
- Niche genre enthusiasts are highly engaged but hard to find
- Traditional algorithmic recommendations favor popular music

---

## Instagram for Professional Networking

**Prompt:**
Instagram wants to compete with LinkedIn for professional networking. Design the experience.

**Constraint:** Cannot make Instagram feel like "work" or alienate existing users

**Context:**
- Instagram has 2B+ MAU, mostly personal/lifestyle content
- Professional creators already use Instagram for business
- LinkedIn dominates professional networking but lacks visual storytelling

---

## Airbnb Post-Checkout Experience

**Prompt:**
Design what happens after a guest checks out of an Airbnb.

**Constraint:** Must generate incremental revenue without making guests feel nickeled-and-dimed

**Context:**
- Post-checkout is currently just review requests
- Guests often want to return to areas they've visited
- Airbnb takes 15% commission on bookings

---

## Google Maps for Indoor Navigation

**Prompt:**
Google wants to bring Maps navigation inside large buildings (airports, malls, hospitals). Figure it out.

**Constraint:** Cannot require building owners to install new infrastructure

**Context:**
- GPS doesn't work reliably indoors
- Large venues lose millions in "wayfinding time" annually
- Users already use Google Maps to find venues, but get lost inside

---

## Duolingo for Professional Skills

**Prompt:**
Duolingo wants to teach professional skills (like Excel, SQL, public speaking) using their gamification approach.

**Constraint:** Must maintain Duolingo's core "5 minutes a day" experience

**Context:**
- Professional skills courses typically require hours of commitment
- Duolingo's strength is bite-sized, addictive lessons
- Corporate learning market is $370B annually

---

## Tips for Using These Prompts:

1. **Don't immediately jump to solutions** - Spend time in Discovery asking clarifying questions
2. **Challenge the constraint** - Understand WHY the constraint exists
3. **Identify the business goal** - What's the company trying to achieve?
4. **Map the users** - Who are ALL the stakeholders affected by this?
5. **Explore widely before converging** - Generate multiple approaches before picking one
6. **Think in systems** - What are the edge cases, errors, and failure modes?
7. **Be opinionated** - Take a stance and defend it with reasoning
