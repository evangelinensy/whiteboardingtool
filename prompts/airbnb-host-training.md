# Airbnb Host Training System

## Challenge

You lead a global team at Airbnb.

**How could the platform teach hosts to provide safer, cleaner, and more welcoming stays without extensive required training?**

## Target Users: Hosts

### Segments

**Experience Level:**
- First-time Airbnb hosts
- Seasoned Airbnb hosts

**Property Type:**
- Large real estate portfolios
- Small real estate (1-2 properties)
- Own the homes
- Rent the homes

**Demographics:**
- Different languages they speak
- Tech-savvy vs. non-tech-savvy
- Various locations

**Geography:**
- International (developed countries with good wifi access)
- Cities
- Rural areas
- Developing countries (remote areas with low internet access)
- Hard-to-reach or difficult-to-navigate locations

**Accessibility & Safety Considerations:**
- Different safety standards across regions
- Cultural differences in hospitality norms

## Problems to Solve

### Core Issues:
- Hosts and guests may have different expectations for standards of a "good stay" (e.g., clean, safe, welcoming)
- Different definitions of what is "safe," "clean," or "welcoming"
- Cultural influences on hospitality standards
- Geographical variations in norms and resources

### Why This Matters for Hosts:
- Satisfied customers
- Good reviews
- More future customers
- Better reputation on the platform

### Why This Matters for Airbnb:
- Saves cost for training programs
- Profit margin increase
- Faster go-to-market (GTM) for new stays
- Improved platform quality and trust

## Potential Solution Approaches

**Delivery Methods:**
- Video training
- VR/AR experiences
- Phone calls
- In-person sessions
- Short-form content (like YouTube Shorts, TikTok)
- Guides and documentation

## Success Criteria

A successful solution should:
- Scale globally across different regions and internet connectivity levels
- Accommodate hosts with varying tech literacy
- Address cultural and geographical differences in standards
- Be cost-effective for Airbnb
- Drive measurable improvement in guest satisfaction
- Minimize training time required from hosts
- Provide clear, actionable guidance on safety, cleanliness, and hospitality

## Constraints

- Must work in low-bandwidth environments
- Cannot require extensive mandatory training (hosts won't complete it)
- Must respect cultural differences while maintaining minimum standards
- Should be accessible in multiple languages
- Budget-conscious (minimize cost per host trained)
